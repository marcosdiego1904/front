## 1. What Is Lamp to My Feet?

Lamp to My Feet is an interactive, gamified web application that helps Christians memorize Bible verses through a structured, multi-sensory learning method. The name comes from Psalm 119:105: *"Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path."*

The core promise: **Memorize any Bible verse in 10 minutes — remember it forever.**

The app transforms Bible study from passive reading into an active, rewarding, and spiritually enriching experience using proven memorization techniques and motivational design patterns.

### Core Pillars
- **Learn** — Effective tools to understand and memorize Scripture
- **Live** — Encouraging real-life application of verses
- **Shine** — Motivating users to grow in faith and track their spiritual journey

## 3. The Problem We Solve

Most Christians want to know the Bible more deeply but struggle with memorization. The statistics framed on the platform:

- **87% of Christians can't recall a single verse** when facing real-life struggles like anxiety, tough decisions, or temptation.
- **Those who memorize Scripture report 3x higher peace and clarity** in daily challenges.

The app positions Scripture memorization not as academic exercise, but as **practical life equipment** — a weapon for daily battles. The messaging frames it as going from "unarmed" (anxiety overwhelms, decisions feel impossible, temptation wins) to "armed" (peace replaces anxiety, clarity guides decisions, strength defeats temptation).

### 4.3. The Memorization Method (5-Step Learning Funnel)

This is the core product. Every verse goes through a science-backed, multi-sensory learning sequence designed to move the verse from short-term to long-term memory. The entire process takes approximately **5–10 minutes**.

**Step 1: Introduction — "Get Ready"**
- Displays the full verse with its reference
- Provides context and meaning behind the verse
- Explains what's coming next in the learning process
- Collapsible section explains *"How you'll remember this forever"* with a motivational overview
- Sets user expectations: *"In 5 minutes, you'll have memorized this verse so deeply you'll remember it years from now."*

**Step 2: Read Aloud — "Read & Lock In"**
- Displays the verse in large, readable text
- Instructs the user to read it **out loud at least 3 times**
- Explains the science: *"Reading aloud activates both visual and auditory pathways in your brain. You'll remember this 2x better than if you just read it silently."*
- This step engages auditory memory on top of visual

**Step 3: Verse Breakdown — "Break It Down"**
- Splits the verse into smaller, digestible fragments (by natural punctuation breaks)
- User navigates through each fragment one at a time with a progress bar
- Reduces cognitive load by focusing on meaning piece by piece
- Instructions: *"Read each fragment below. Focus on what it means. This is how your brain remembers forever."*

**Step 4: Fill in the Blanks — "Prove It"**
- Presents the verse with strategic words replaced by blank input fields (approximately every 4th word)
- User types the missing words from memory
- Real-time feedback: correct answers turn green, incorrect turn red
- Accepts answers regardless of punctuation or capitalization
- User can only proceed when all blanks are filled correctly
- This is the first active recall test — the critical moment where memory solidifies

**Step 5: Write from Memory — "Master It"**
- The ultimate test: user writes the entire verse from memory with no hints
- Real-time word-by-word feedback as they type (green for correct, red for incorrect)
- Can only proceed when the entire verse is written correctly
- The button reads: *"I've Mastered This"* — only activates on 100% accuracy
- This final recall effort cements the verse into long-term memory

**Step 6: Completion — Celebration Screen**
- Headline: *"You Just Did What 90% of People Can't"*
- Reinforces the achievement: *"You didn't just read [verse] — you mastered it. This verse is now locked in your memory."*
- For logged-in users: verse is automatically saved to their personal collection
- For non-logged-in users: strong conversion prompt — *"You're about to lose this progress"* with a free signup CTA
- Options: Go Home, Restart Lesson, or View Profile

**Visual Progress Tracker:** Throughout all steps, a visual progress indicator shows numbered steps (1–6) with checkmarks for completed steps and a progress bar connecting them.

### 4.4. User Accounts & Dashboard

**Registration & Login:** Standard email-based authentication with password recovery.

**Dashboard (Post-Login Home):** A personalized hub showing:
- Welcome message with user's name
- **Three stat badges:** Current Rank, Learning Streak (in days), Verses Memorized
- **"Ready for Your Next Verse?"** — Primary call-to-action to continue learning
- **Recently Memorized** — Last 5 verses with dates
- **Suggested For You** — AI-recommended verse based on categories the user hasn't explored, filtered to exclude already-memorized verses
- **This Week Summary** — Verses memorized this week, day streak, progress to next rank
- **Quick Links** — Browse Verses, View Journey, My Collection

### 4.5. Memorized Verses Collection ("My Verses")

A personal library of every verse the user has memorized, with:
- **Search bar** to find specific verses
- **Filter by book** of the Bible
- **Sort options:** Newest First, Oldest First, A–Z
- **Statistics panel:** Total verses, current rank, books covered, Old Testament count, New Testament count, most memorized book
- **Expandable verse cards:** Shows reference, preview text, date memorized; expands to show full text and a "Practice Again" button
- This is the user's "treasure box" — a growing collection that demonstrates their spiritual journey

The app uses a **Biblical Journey ranking system** with 8 levels, each named after a biblical figure whose story represents that stage of spiritual growth:

| Rank | Verses Required | Icon | Theme |
|------|----------------|------|-------|
| **Nicodemus** | 1–3 | Lantern | *"You've come seeking truth. This is where transformation begins."* |
| **Thomas** | 4–8 | Wave/Hello | *"You're building your faith one verse at a time. Doubt isn't weakness — it's the path to unshakeable belief."* |
| **Peter** | 9–16 | Boat | *"You've left the boat. You're walking on water, learning to trust."* |
| **John** | 17–27 | Heart | *"You're drawing close. Scripture is becoming personal."* |
| **Paul** | 28–40 | Light | *"What began as knowledge is now part of who you are. You're a new creation."* |
| **David** | 41–55 | Heart | *"You don't just know the Word — you sing it, dance it, live it. A heart after God's own."* |
| **Daniel** | 56–75 | Lion | *"Nothing shakes you. Your faith is unshakeable."* |
| **Solomon** | 76–100+ | Crown | *"You possess wisdom beyond measure. You are among the elite few who have climbed this mountain."* |

Each rank includes:
- A percentage-based progress bar within the level
- Calculation of verses needed to reach the next rank
- Level-up notifications when the user advances
- The ranking is visible on the dashboard, profile, and collection pages

### 4.7. User Profile

Users can view and manage their profile:
- **Avatar** with initials, display name, username, member-since date
- **Quick stats:** Total verses, current rank, progress to next rank
- **Editable fields:** First name, last name, biography
- **Non-editable fields:** Username, email (displayed but locked)
- **Quick action cards:** My Verses, View Journey, Browse Verses
- **Account management:** Logout, Delete Account (with confirmation dialog)

### 4.8. Leaderboard / Ranking Journey

A page where users can view their progression through the biblical ranking system, see their current level, and understand what's needed to advance. Includes level-up notifications and a visual representation of their journey.
